Eyes on the road

Breath Easy

Pick a pattern. Start it. Put the phone down.

Not while driving

Left out on purpose: Wim Hof rounds, breath of fire and kapalabhati, Buteyko air-hunger holds, anything with a retention past about 10 seconds, and alternate-nostril breathing — the first four can grey out your vision, and the last one wants a hand off the wheel.

Breathe through the nose, jaw loose, belly moving rather than shoulders. Light-headed or tingly: stop the pattern and breathe normally.

If the car stays quiet
  1. Set the head unit source to Bluetooth Audio or USB — a phone paired only for calls won't carry it.
  2. On iPhone, flip the ring/silent switch to ring.
  3. Raise volume while a cue is actually playing, or the phone adjusts the ringer instead.
  4. Add this page to your Home Screen and open it from there. It then holds the screen awake properly and works with no signal.
Not medical advice. Runs entirely on your device — nothing is sent anywhere.
